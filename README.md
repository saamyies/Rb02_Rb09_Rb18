### Project Description:
Blackjack is a classic casino game wherein players play against their dealer--in this case, the program--to get a hand as close to 21 points without going over. This is played with the standard deck of 52 cards.

Number cards from 2-10 have points worth at their face value.
Face cards [ JACK, QUEEN, KING ] are worth 10 points.
Ace cards are worth either 1 or 11 points; if the sum of the previous cards are less than or equal to ten, then its points are equivalent to 11. Otherwise, an ace is worth 1 point.

<img width="738" height="306" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/538be602-97ec-4bc3-bfd8-1032101dac9b" />

To start a round, the dealer deals two cards for each of them; for the dealer, one of their cards is hidden from the player.
Then, the player checks for the sum of the two cards; if the sum of the points is 21, they automatically win unless the dealer also has 21 points, then it would result in a tie, and the round ends.
The player can choose to either hit or stand:
  Hit - To draw another card from the deck. The player may hit as many times, however going over 21 points is a bust, meaning they lose the round.
  Stand - To commit to your current total and end your turn.
Once the player is done with their turns, the dealer reveals the hidden card. If the dealer's total is greater than the player's, the dealer wins. If the player's total is greater than the dealer's, then the player wins. If they are equal, then it is a tie.

If the player loses, the dealer may give a dare to the player for them to do.
